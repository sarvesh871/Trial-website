1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To develop a hiring and culture plan that enables a startup to scale its team from approximately 10 employees to 100+ while preserving organisational culture and effectiveness.

Learning Outcomes

  • Design a role-based hiring roadmap aligned to business growth milestones.
  • Identify culture risks that emerge during rapid headcount growth.
  • Propose structures and practices (onboarding, communication, values reinforcement) that preserve culture at scale.

2 Background / Theory

Growing from a 10-person founding team to 100+ employees changes an organisation qualitatively, not just numerically — informal coordination breaks down, culture that was 'lived' by founders must be deliberately codified, and management layers become necessary. This exercise applies:

  • Dunbar’s Number Thresholds: Cognitive limits on direct social relationships (~15 for intimate trust, ~50 for informal coordination, ~150 for mutual recognition). Beyond 50 employees, informal hallway alignment fails.
  • Culture Codification Concept: Translating implicit founder values into written operational guidelines, performance criteria, and onboarding rituals before rapid hiring dilutes the company's core mission.
  • Generalist to Specialist Transition: Moving from early generalist problem-solvers (who wear multiple hats) to disciplined functional specialists with deep domain mastery (QA automation, compliance, logistics).

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • Organisational design template: Prescribed in Annexure G.
  • Sample hiring roadmap template: Role profiles, headcount allocations, and milestone mapping.

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Define current team structure: Using the chosen startup (Solvanta), define current team structure (roles, reporting lines) at ~10 employees.
  2. 2
    Define target headcount across 3 milestones: Define target functional areas and headcount at three milestones: 25, 50, and 100+ employees, mapped to business milestones (e.g., new product line, new market, production scale-up).
  3. 3
    Specify critical roles & profiles: For each milestone, specify the critical roles to hire, required skill profiles, and whether hires are generalist or specialist.
  4. 4
    Identify at least four culture risks: Identify at least four culture risks associated with rapid scaling (e.g., loss of informal communication, dilution of founding values, inconsistent decision-making, onboarding overload).
  5. 5
    Propose concrete mitigations: E.g., a documented culture handbook, structured onboarding programme, manager training, regular all-hands practices, and feedback mechanisms.
  6. 6
    Summarise scaling roadmap: Summarise the complete plan as an organisational scaling roadmap with a timeline.

6 Four Culture Scaling Risks & Concrete Mitigations

Culture Risk 01

Loss of Informal Communication

Information shared organically around coffee machines fails to reach remote field teams and new hires, causing functional blindness.

Concrete Mitigation: Codified asynchronous communication norms, bi-weekly virtual town halls with open Q&A, and centralized documentation in a company wiki.
Culture Risk 02

Dilution of Founding Values

Rapid hiring brings employees who treat Solvanta as a standard corporate job, losing the founding obsession with clean-tech sustainability.

Concrete Mitigation: Documented Solvanta Culture Playbook; values-based behavioral interview rubrics; founder-led session during week one of onboarding.
Culture Risk 03

Inconsistent Management Styles

First-time managers hired externally apply divergent, micromanaging, or opaque leadership habits, creating employee discontent.

Concrete Mitigation: Mandatory internal "First-Time Manager Academy", standardized 1-on-1 coaching frameworks, and quarterly anonymous 360-degree upward reviews.
Culture Risk 04

Onboarding Overload & Burnout

Existing senior engineers spend 50% of their working hours answering repetitive basic questions, stalling core technical product delivery.

Concrete Mitigation: Structured 30-60-90 day onboarding curriculum, dedicated peer "culture buddies", and self-serve interactive technical walkthroughs.
