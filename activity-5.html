1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To identify the key risks a startup faces while scaling and to prepare contingency plans and resilience strategies to manage them.

Learning Outcomes

  • Identify and classify scaling-stage risks across operational, financial, market, and regulatory categories.
  • Quantify risk using a probability–impact framework.
  • Design mitigation and contingency strategies, including early-warning indicators.

2 Background / Theory

Scaling amplifies risk: a supplier issue that was a minor inconvenience at pilot scale can halt production entirely at full scale. Structured risk assessment uses a probability–impact matrix to prioritise risks, followed by:

  • Mitigation planning: Preventive actions that proactively reduce the likelihood (probability) of occurrence.
  • Contingency planning: Pre-authorized fallback actions that reduce the impact if the risk materialises.
  • Resilience strategies: Operational redundancies that enable faster systemic recovery and business continuity.

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • Risk register template: Prescribed in Annexure E.
  • Probability–Impact matrix template: Standard 5×5 scoring grid.

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Brainstorm at least 12 risks: Using the venture from Exercises 1–4, identify at least 12 risks spanning operational (supply/production), financial (funding/cash flow), market (demand/competition), regulatory/compliance, and organisational (talent/culture) categories.
  2. 2
    Score on Probability & Impact: Score each risk on Probability (1–5) and Impact (1–5); plot on a Probability–Impact matrix.
  3. 3
    Prioritise top 6 risks: Prioritise the top 6 risks by risk score (Probability × Impact).
  4. 4
    Formulate three-part response plan: For each prioritised risk, define: a mitigation action (reduces likelihood), a contingency plan (reduces impact if the risk materialises), and an early-warning indicator/trigger metric.
  5. 5
    Assign owner & review frequency: Assign an owner (role, not name) and an explicit review frequency for each risk.
  6. 6
    Compile Risk Mitigation Roadmap: Compile the completed risk register and matrix into a comprehensive Risk Mitigation Roadmap.

6 Top 6 Prioritized Risks: Mitigation, Contingency & Triggers

As required by step 4 of the laboratory procedure, every top-tier risk (Score ≥ 12) is detailed with proactive mitigation, reactive contingency, early-warning trigger indicators, and assigned organizational roles:

Rank 1 | Score 20 (P:4, I:5) Operational

R1: Critical LFP Battery Cell Supply Delay

Proactive Mitigation: Execute dual-source off-take contract with certified secondary supplier; maintain 45-day regional safety stock buffer.
Contingency Plan: Authorize emergency air freight allocation; trigger pre-negotiated priority slot with domestic cell refabricator.
🚨 Trigger Metric: Supplier lead time notification exceeding 12 weeks.
Owner: Head of Operations Review: Bi-Weekly
Rank 2 | Score 16 (P:4, I:4) Financial

R2: Working Capital Cash Flow Crunch

Proactive Mitigation: Secure $2.5M revolving bank credit facility; structure 50% upfront deposits on commercial contracts.
Contingency Plan: Draw down revolving working capital line; defer non-critical capital expenditures.
🚨 Trigger Metric: Cash runway dropping below 4.5 months of operational burn.
Owner: VP Finance / CFO Review: Weekly
Rank 3 | Score 15 (P:3, I:5) Regulatory

R3: Grid Interconnection Certification Delays

Proactive Mitigation: Pre-compliance testing at accredited third-party laboratories; engage regional utility standards taskforce.
Contingency Plan: Offer islanded off-grid mode firmware configuration while grid-tie certification is processed.
🚨 Trigger Metric: Standards body review cycle exceeding 90 days without audit date.
Owner: Regulatory & Compliance Lead Review: Monthly
Rank 4 | Score 12 (P:4, I:3) Market

R4: Aggressive Competitor OEM Price War

Proactive Mitigation: Differentiate with Solvanta OS smart telemetry, 99.4% uptime SLA, and circular battery buyback guarantee.
Contingency Plan: Launch zero-capex Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) leasing model to remove upfront purchasing barriers.
🚨 Trigger Metric: Competitor discount margin exceeding 20% on tier-1 bids.
Owner: Chief Commercial Officer Review: Monthly
Rank 5 | Score 12 (P:3, I:4) Organisational

R5: Shortage of Battery Firmware Engineers

Proactive Mitigation: Competitive equity compensation; university clean-tech partnerships; structured onboarding accelerator.
Contingency Plan: Retain pre-vetted specialized embedded firmware engineering consultancy for surge capacity.
🚨 Trigger Metric: Key technical engineering vacancies open for > 60 days.
Owner: Head of People & Culture Review: Monthly
Rank 6 | Score 12 (P:3, I:4) Operational

R6: Quality Defect in Contract Mfg Batch

Proactive Mitigation: Deploy full-time Solvanta QA engineer on-site at contract facility; mandate Automated Optical Inspection (AOI).
Contingency Plan: Halt batch dispatch; isolate affected lot numbers via digital component serial tracking.
🚨 Trigger Metric: First-pass manufacturing yield falling below 96.5%.
Owner: Lead Quality Engineer Review: Weekly
