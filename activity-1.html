Environmental Dimension Parameters (Planet)

1. Raw Material Sourcing & Recycled Content

Proportion of certified recycled aluminum alloy used for PowerBlock enclosures vs virgin high-carbon smelted metal.

Evidence: Current enclosure uses 35% recycled alloy; target 100%.

2. Manufacturing Energy Intensity & Renewables

Grid power source utilized during manual battery pack cell balancing and electrical QA burn-in cycles.

Evidence: Workshop runs on regional grid (58% coal-powered); zero rooftop solar installed.

3. Packaging & In-Transit Dunnage Waste

Single-use expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam cushioning vs standardized reusable circular crates.

Evidence: Current freight uses unrecyclable foam blocks and heavy plastic stretch film.

4. End-of-Life Battery Take-Back & Circularity

Contractual commitments and physical logistics for reclaiming degraded LFP cells after their 6,000-cycle life.

Evidence: No formal take-back channel established for 120 pilot units; informal disposal risk.

Social & Community Parameters (People)

1. High-Voltage Occupational Safety & PPE Standards

Protocols, arc-flash protection, and certified training for assembly technicians handling 51.2V/100Ah battery banks.

Evidence: Basic PPE available, but lack formalized ISO 45001 safety checklists in garage workshop.

2. Fair Compensation & Living Wage Verification

Remuneration benchmarking for junior assembly technicians and field installation contractors.

Evidence: Full-time employees earn 18% above regional living wage; contractor rates vary.

3. Workforce Diversity & Technical Inclusivity

Gender balance, equal pay audit, and underrepresented talent inclusion across hardware engineering roles.

Evidence: Currently only 2 of 11 full-time team members are women; lack formal diversity pipeline.

4. Community Energy Access & Local Installer Training

Capacity building for local electricians in peri-urban and agricultural districts to service micro-grids.

Evidence: Trained 14 local agro-electricians during pilot; high community enthusiasm.

Economic & Financial Viability (Profit)

1. Unit Contribution Margin Viability

Hardware margin after factoring direct lithium cells, inverter electronics, and manual assembly hours.

Evidence: Healthy initial margin of 42.2% ($1,900 gross margin on $4,500 selling price).

2. Working Capital & Cash Runway Resilience

Vulnerability to 16-week cell supplier prepayments and delayed receivables from commercial clients.

Evidence: High vulnerability; only 4.2 months cash runway at current burn rate.

3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to LTV Ratio

Founder-led sales efficiency vs recurring value generated through Solvanta OS IoT subscriptions.

Evidence: High CAC ($1,200) due to custom site surveys; LTV currently capped by one-off sale.

4. Single-Source Supply Chain Price Resilience

Exposure to global lithium carbonate commodity price spikes and single-vendor contract locking.

Evidence: High exposure; 100% of cells sourced from a single Tier-2 supplier without price hedge.

1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To critically evaluate an existing startup (or the student's own venture idea) across environmental, social, and economic dimensions, and to identify gaps and improvement opportunities using a structured audit framework.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply the Triple Bottom Line (People, Planet, Profit) framework to a real or hypothetical startup.
  • Identify sustainability gaps in current operations, sourcing, and business practices.
  • Propose actionable, feasible improvements aligned with sustainability principles.

2 Background / Theory

Sustainability audits assess how well an organisation balances environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and economic viability. For early-stage startups, sustainability is often treated as an afterthought; this exercise trains students to build it into the diagnostic process from the outset, using recognised frameworks such as the Triple Bottom Line, the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and lifecycle thinking.

In engineering and clean-tech ventures like Solvanta, environmental impact is embedded directly in hardware components (embodied carbon in raw aluminum, rare earth extraction for battery cells, toxic end-of-life disposal). Social sustainability touches occupational safety during high-voltage assembly and community capacity building. Economic sustainability ensures that the venture generates adequate contribution margins without burning through cash reserves before scale is achieved.

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • Startup profile: Chosen company website/annual report, or the student's own business idea (e.g. Solvanta Energy Systems Case Dossier).
  • Sustainability Audit Checklist: Prescribed rubric in Annexure A.
  • Disclosures: Access to public ESG/CSR disclosures, if available.

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Select a startup for analysis: Either a real company (preferably in the mechanical, automotive, energy, or manufacturing space) or the student's own venture concept (or use Solvanta Energy Systems).
  2. 2
    Gather background information: Business model, products/services, target market, and current scale of operations.
  3. 3
    Conduct the audit under three heads: Environmental (energy/material use, waste, emissions), Social (labour practices, community impact, diversity), and Economic (revenue model, cost structure, financial resilience).
  4. 4
    Score each parameter: Score each parameter on a 1–5 scale using the rubric in Annexure A and document supporting evidence for each score.
  5. 5
    Identify specific gaps: Identify at least five specific gaps across the three dimensions (e.g. where current performance scores ≤ 2).
  6. 6
    Propose concrete improvements: Propose at least one concrete, resourced improvement for each identified gap, noting expected impact and implementation cost/effort.
  7. 7
    Summarise findings: Summarise findings in a one-page executive summary suitable for founder/investor review.

5 Deliverables / Submission (Annexure A Reference)

Your laboratory report submission must include the following four items:

  • Completed Sustainability Audit Checklist with scores and supporting evidence.
  • Gap analysis table (minimum 5 gaps identified).
  • Improvement proposal table mapped to each identified gap.
  • One-page executive summary for founder or investor review.
