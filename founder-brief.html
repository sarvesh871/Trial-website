Company Profile

Solvanta Energy Systems, Inc.

"Modular Clean Power for a Resilient World"
Seed Scale-Up

Solvanta designs and manufactures the PowerBlock™, a plug-and-play modular solar micro-inverter and smart battery energy storage system (5kWh to 50kWh stackable capacity). The system enables commercial enterprises in weak-grid or off-grid environments to replace expensive, carbon-heavy diesel generators with reliable clean power.

Industry
Clean-Tech / Renewable Hardware & IoT
Current Team Size
11 Full-Time Employees
Primary Business Model
Direct B2B Hardware + IoT SaaS Telemetry
Target Customers
Commercial Agribusiness, Cold Chains, Clinics
Hardware Architecture PowerBlock™ Gen 2

Modular Stackable Energy System

An integrated lithium iron phosphate (LFP) enclosure with bidirectional hybrid inverter and cellular IoT edge gateway.

Rated Output: 5.0 kW AC Continuous Recyclable Aluminum Casing
Operating Baseline

Current Operational Metrics & Constraints

Where Solvanta stands today after its initial 120-unit regional pilot deployment.

Monthly Assembly Ceiling
35 units / mo

Constrained by manual wiring and in-house bench testing across 1,200 sq ft rented garage workshop.

Battery Cell Lead Time
16 Weeks

Single-sourced imported lithium iron phosphate supplier creates acute working capital and scheduling fragility.

Unit Economics (Pilot)
$4,500 / $2,600

Unit Selling Price ($4,500) vs Variable BOM & direct labor ($2,600); contribution margin is $1,900 (42.2%).

Current Deployed Footprint
120 Units

Installed across 18 regional micro-enterprises, dairy chillers, and peri-urban rural health clinics.

Target Growth Milestones

Year 1 (Pilot Consolidation):

Achieve 500 units/year; transition to ISO 9001 contract manufacturing; reach $2.25M gross revenue.

Year 2–3 (Scale-Up Phase):

Scale to 3,000 units/year; launch Solvanta OS installer partner marketplace; achieve company break-even.

Year 4–5 (Mass Production):

Surpass 25,000 units/year; expand into 3 international regional hubs; launch certified circular recycling facility.

Core Sustainability Imperatives

Decarbonize Supply Chain (Scope 3):

Current aluminum casing has high embodied carbon. Transition to 100% recycled low-carbon aluminum and eliminate diesel freight.

Closed-Loop Circular Battery Take-Back:

Implement a guaranteed end-of-life battery passport and recycling credit system to recover 95% of active cathode lithium/cobalt.

Fair Labor & Technical Capacity Equity:

Establish certified regional training programs for local women and youth installers, ensuring safe, high-voltage certified careers.
