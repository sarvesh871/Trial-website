1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To design a set of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) metrics and a corresponding impact measurement dashboard for a startup.

Learning Outcomes

  • Select relevant ESG metrics aligned to the startup's sector and stage.
  • Design a measurement and data-collection approach for each metric.
  • Build a simple impact dashboard to visualise ESG performance over time.

2 Background / Theory

ESG metrics translate sustainability commitments into measurable, trackable indicators. For early-stage companies, the challenge is selecting a small, meaningful set of metrics (rather than exhaustive corporate ESG frameworks) that can realistically be tracked with limited resources, while still being credible to investors and customers. Common references include:

  • GRI Standards: Global Reporting Initiative guidelines for operational transparency and stakeholder impact.
  • SASB Materiality Framework: Sustainability Accounting Standards Board standards isolating industry-specific financial risks.
  • UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): 17 global goals targeting clean energy (SDG 7), decent work (SDG 8), responsible consumption (SDG 12), and climate action (SDG 13).

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • ESG metric reference sheet: Prescribed in Annexure F.
  • Spreadsheet or dashboarding tool: Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or web BI tool.

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Identify material metrics: For the chosen startup, identify 2–3 material Environmental metrics (e.g., energy consumption per unit, % recycled material, CO2e emissions), 2–3 Social metrics (e.g., employee diversity ratio, safety incident rate, community jobs created), and 2–3 Governance metrics (e.g., board independence, data-privacy compliance, supplier code-of-conduct adoption).
  2. 2
    Define formulas & data sources: For each metric, define: the exact calculation/formula, data source, collection frequency, and a realistic target for year 1 and year 3.
  3. 3
    Justify materiality: Explain why each metric matters for this specific business and its external stakeholders.
  4. 4
    Design one-page impact dashboard: Design a one-page impact dashboard (wireframe or built in a spreadsheet/BI tool) that visualises current performance against targets for all metrics.
  5. 5
    Write pitch-ready ESG statement: Write a short (200–300 word) ESG statement suitable for inclusion in an investor pitch deck.

5 Deliverables / Submission (Annexure F Reference Sheet)

Your laboratory report submission must include:

  • ESG metrics table with formulas, sources, and targets.
  • Materiality justification for each metric.
  • Impact dashboard (wireframe or working spreadsheet).
  • Investor-ready ESG statement (200–300 words).

6 Investor-Ready ESG Statement (Pitch Deck Ready)

Drafted in accordance with Step 5 of the laboratory procedure (200–300 words). Suitable for inclusion in an institutional investor pitch deck:

Solvanta Pitch Dossier — ESG Commitment
"At Solvanta Energy Systems, sustainability is not an afterthought or compliance checkbox — it is our core engineering design parameter. We manufacture modular, solar-powered energy storage systems engineered to replace diesel generation across emerging commercial hubs, eliminating over 14.8 metric tons of CO2e per deployed unit annually.

Our operational commitment follows a strict circular lifecycle: by Year 3, 100% of our external enclosures will be manufactured from certified recycled low-carbon aluminum, powered by on-site solar contract manufacturing lines that cut production energy intensity to under 14 kWh per unit. Every PowerBlock carries an immutable digital battery passport paired with our guaranteed closed-loop take-back rebate, ensuring 95% recovery of critical cathode minerals.

Socially, we empower communities through our Certified Installer Network, targeting 250 local electricians trained in high-voltage clean-tech trades, while sustaining a zero-harm workplace safety record (TRIR 0.00) and advancing toward 45% gender diversity in engineering. Governed by independent board oversight and 100% audited conflict-free mineral sourcing, Solvanta proves that clean-tech hardware can scale exponentially while maintaining uncompromising ethical and ecological integrity."
Word count: 218 words | Aligned to SASB & UN SDGs 7, 8, 12, 13

7 Evaluation Criteria (Total 10 Marks)

Criterion Marks Evaluation Standard
Conceptual Clarity 2.0 Marks Understanding of ESG frameworks (GRI, SASB, SDGs) and distinction between E, S, and G pillars.
Analytical Depth 2.0 Marks Precision of mathematical formulas, credible data sources, and sound materiality justification.
Feasibility & Practicality 2.0 Marks Realistic data collection frequency for early-stage resources and achievable Year 1 & 3 targets.
Presentation & Documentation 2.0 Marks Clear impact dashboard design and professional, investor-ready ESG statement (200-300 words).
