Three Embedded Sustainability Interventions

As required by the lab syllabus, at least three circular interventions must be designed directly into the operations plan:

1. Circular Recycled Material Sourcing

Transition PowerBlock external extrusions to 100% post-consumer recycled aluminum alloy (reducing embodied smelting energy by 95%), coupled with tier-2 conflict-free cobalt audit certification.

2. Zero-Waste Returnable Dunnage

Eliminate 100% of single-use expanded polystyrene foam by adopting standardized rotomolded cellular shipping crates that circulate between certified installation contractors and regional 3PL hubs.

3. QR Battery Passport & Take-Back

Every LFP module carries an immutable QR digital battery passport tracking cycle health. A $150 end-of-life core rebate incentivizes customers to return expired packs to certified hydrometallurgical recycling partners.

1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To develop a scalable supply chain and operations strategy, with an explicit sustainability focus, for a product involving manufacturing, automotive components, or energy devices.

Learning Outcomes

  • Map an end-to-end supply chain for the chosen product category.
  • Identify scaling constraints in sourcing, production, logistics, and inventory.
  • Design a phased operations scaling plan that embeds sustainability practices (e.g., green sourcing, waste reduction, reverse logistics).

2 Background / Theory

Operations that work at prototype or pilot scale frequently break down at 10x or 100x volume due to supplier capacity limits, quality-control bottlenecks, logistics costs, and inventory carrying costs. A sustainable scaling plan anticipates these breakpoints and designs around them using strategies such as dual sourcing, modular production cells, just-in-time (JIT) inventory, and circular material flows.

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • Product specification sheet: Chosen by student/team (e.g. Solvanta PowerBlock™ 5kWh Modular Storage).
  • Supply chain mapping template: Prescribed in Annexure C.
  • Reference data: Supplier lead times and capacities (approximate/assumed where unavailable).

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Select a product: Involving manufacturing, an automotive component, or an energy device (e.g., a battery pack, an alternator, a solar micro-inverter) — ideally a product made by your chosen startup.
  2. 2
    Map the current/baseline supply chain: Raw material sourcing, tier-1/tier-2 suppliers, production stages, warehousing, and distribution to end customer.
  3. 3
    Identify volume breakpoints: Identify the production volume at which each stage becomes a bottleneck (capacity, quality, or cost) as demand scales from pilot to mass production.
  4. 4
    Design a scaling strategy: Addressing sourcing (single vs. dual/multi-sourcing), production (in-house vs. contract manufacturing, automation level), logistics (warehousing network, transport mode), and inventory (JIT vs. buffer stock).
  5. 5
    Embed at least three sustainability interventions: E.g., recycled/low-carbon material sourcing, packaging reduction, reverse logistics/take-back scheme, renewable-powered facilities.
  6. 6
    Present as a phased roadmap: Present the plan as a phased roadmap (Pilot → Scale-up → Mass Production) with volume thresholds and corresponding operational changes at each phase.

5 Deliverables / Submission (Annexure C Roadmap)

Your lab submission must contain:

  • Baseline supply chain map.
  • Bottleneck analysis identifying breakpoints by volume.
  • Phased scaling roadmap with embedded sustainability interventions.

Annexure C: Complete Operational Scaling Matrix

Operations Dimension Phase 1: Pilot (100 u/mo) Phase 2: Scale-Up (2,500 u/mo) Phase 3: Mass Production (25,000 u/mo)
Sourcing Strategy Single vendor; spot cell purchases; 16-wk lead times. Dual-sourcing for battery cells & BMS chips; 60-day vendor contracts. Multi-regional procurement; long-term mineral off-take; local assembly kits.
Production Setup 1,200 sq ft garage; manual bench soldering (35 u/mo cap). Contract Manufacturer (CM); automated cell welding & AOI testing. Fully automated robotic line (ISO 9001/14001); lights-out cell assembly.
Logistics & Freight Direct road freight; diesel LTL trucks; single warehouse. Regional 3PL fulfillment depots; route optimization software. Intermodal electric road + rail; localized regional distribution centers.
Inventory Strategy 45-day safety buffer due to high lead time risk. Hybrid JIT for casings; 20-day safety stock for imported cells. Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI) with IoT real-time replenishment.
Sustainability Focus Basic scrap sorting; virgin aluminum enclosure. 100% recycled aluminum; reusable returnable shipping crates. Net-zero solar factory; 95% circular battery take-back hydrometallurgy.
