1 Objective & Learning Outcomes

Objective: To redesign the Business Model Canvas (BMC) of a mechanical/engineering-based startup so that it can scale efficiently, incorporating automation, outsourcing, and/or platform-based elements.

Learning Outcomes

  • Construct a baseline Business Model Canvas for an engineering-based startup.
  • Diagnose structural constraints that limit scalability in the baseline model.
  • Redesign the canvas to embed automation, outsourcing, or platform mechanisms that enable scale without proportional cost growth.

2 Background / Theory

Many engineering and manufacturing startups scale linearly — doubling output requires roughly doubling headcount and capital. Scalable business models decouple growth from proportional resource consumption through:

1. Automation

Reducing manual labour dependency through automated testing, robotic assembly, or software workflows.

2. Outsourcing

Converting fixed capital costs to variable unit costs and accessing specialized third-party capacity (e.g. Contract Manufacturing, 3PL).

3. Platform Elements

Enabling network effects, ecosystem partner value creation, and high-margin software telemetry (e.g. installer marketplaces, APIs).

This exercise applies Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas as the diagnostic redesign tool to systematically eliminate linear growth bottlenecks.

3 Materials / Resources Required

  • Blank Business Model Canvas template: Annexure B reference layout.
  • Case brief: Case brief on a mechanical/engineering startup (instructor-provided or self-selected — e.g. Solvanta Energy Systems).

4 Procedure / Tasks

  1. 1
    Select or receive a case brief: For a mechanical/engineering-based startup (e.g., a precision-parts manufacturer, an EV component maker, or a renewable energy device company) — or continue using your Exercise 1 startup if it fits this profile.
  2. 2
    Draft current-state Business Model Canvas: Covering all nine blocks: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, and Cost Structure.
  3. 3
    Analyse each block for scalability bottlenecks: E.g., dependence on skilled manual labour, single-supplier risk, capital-intensive fixed assets.
  4. 4
    Redesign the canvas: Explicitly introduce at least one automation element (e.g., automated QA, robotic assembly), one outsourcing element (e.g., contract manufacturing, 3PL logistics), and one platform element (e.g., a partner marketplace, API-based service layer).
  5. 5
    Justify each redesign choice: Document expected impact on unit cost, lead time, or capacity ceiling.
  6. 6
    Present side by side with a change log: Present the before/after canvases side by side with an itemized change log table.

5 Deliverables / Submission (Annexure B Change Log)

Your laboratory submission must include:

  • Baseline Business Model Canvas.
  • Redesigned (scalable) Business Model Canvas.
  • Change log with explicit justification for each modification.
