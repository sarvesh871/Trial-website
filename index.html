Core Framework

The Triple Bottom Line Paradigm

Sustainable scaling requires balancing People, Planet, and Profit. When a startup optimizes for only one, the venture collapses under hidden externalities.

Planet

Environmental Stewardship

Managing ecological boundaries, carbon intensity, circular material flows, and end-of-life battery recycling across production.

  • Raw material lifecycle & recyclability
  • Manufacturing energy intensity (kWh/unit)
  • Circular reverse logistics & packaging waste

People

Social & Organizational

Cultivating worker safety in battery assembly, fair supplier conditions, community installer capacity, and inclusive team culture.

  • High-voltage workshop safety & training
  • Local technical job creation & installer equity
  • Culture codification past Dunbar’s threshold

Profit

Economic Viability

Achieving strong unit economics, positive contribution margins, self-sustaining break-even volumes, and risk resilience.

  • Robust unit contribution margin (>40%)
  • LTV:CAC ratio exceeding 3.0x at scale
  • Working capital & cash runway management
